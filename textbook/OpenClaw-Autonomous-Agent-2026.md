## 🎯 What is OpenClaw?
- **Open-source autonomous AI agent** — runs on your machine
- **Works from chat apps** — Telegram, WhatsApp, Slack, Discord
- **145k+ GitHub stars** (Feb 2026)
- **Formerly Clawdbot/Moltbot**
- **Local-first, privacy-first** — half a million systems running
