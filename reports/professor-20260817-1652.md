1. **Mutation**: Apply structural mutations to graph nodes/edges (addition, removal, rewiring, rebalancing)
2. **Evaluation**: Score each mutation using fitness functions assessing performance, stability, relevance

- **Community detection**: Modularity clusters within the graph
