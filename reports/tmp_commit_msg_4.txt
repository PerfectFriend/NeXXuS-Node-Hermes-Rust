fix(sound): sporadic soundtrack timers are now cancellable; startSoundtrack is idempotent

Cycle 4 (2026-10-02). Three defects in src/sound/soundSystem.ts, all about the
self-perpetuating sporadic chain (seagulls / crows / bardSong):

1. [СР] scheduleSporadic called `setTimeout(playOnce, ...)` and discarded the
   handle, so stopSoundtrack() could not cancel it. The chain kept
   re-scheduling itself through src.onended: measured, a single stop left 3
   live timers, and 10 minutes of virtual time created 20 MORE timers after
   the music had been switched off. Every toggleMusic(true) stacked three
   more chains on top of the live ones — unbounded timer growth on Android.
   Timers are now tracked in sporadicTimers and cleared in stopSoundtrack().

2. [СР] stopSoundtrack() called layer.source.stop() without clearing
   layer.source.onended first, so stopping each ambient layer scheduled yet
   another timer on the way out. The handler is now detached before stop().

3. [СР] startSoundtrack() was not idempotent: calling it twice (two consecutive
   toggleMusic(true), or two call sites) pushed a second set of ambient layers
   and launched a second triple of chains — music layered over itself and
   timers doubled. It now stops any previous soundtrack first.

Chains additionally carry a generation counter, so a timer that was already
scheduled when stopSoundtrack() ran cannot resurrect its chain.

Adds tests/sound.soundtrack.test.mjs — compiles the REAL soundSystem.ts and
drives it against WebAudio factories plus a hand-rolled virtual clock that
intercepts setTimeout/clearTimeout (the engine is not touched).

Honest note about the test's own first version: the WebAudio factory fired
onended only from stop(), so the "chain must not resurrect after stop"
assertion passed even against the broken code — it was vacuous by
construction. The factory now models a real AudioBufferSourceNode, which
fires onended by itself when the buffer finishes, and the virtual clock
interleaves source completion with timer expiry. After that fix the suite
fails 5 of 7 against the pre-fix source and passes 7/7 after.
