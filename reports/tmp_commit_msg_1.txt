to line 2. Repaired using the canonical marker_guard.marker_safe() as the single
source of truth (marker removed only where the guard says it must never be
written). *.bak/*.backup untouched (rule 3).

failure modes plus a live engine.smoke invocation. Verified non-vacuous: with

passes after restore. Adds test:markers and test:all npm scripts.
