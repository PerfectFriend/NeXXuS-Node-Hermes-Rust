fix(renderer): panning works after pinch, NaN can no longer poison the viewport

Cycle 6 (2026-10-02). Probe: reports/tmp_probe_cycle6.mjs, driving the real
compiled GridRenderer with fabricated canvas/DOM events.

1. [СР] After a pinch, panning with the remaining finger was dead. pointerup
   left isPanning = false, so the finger moved and the field did not
   (measured dx = 0). This is the exact gesture the plan's Point 9 asks about
   ("drag-to-pan works without jitter or lost position") on real hardware:
   zoom with two fingers, then pan with one — nothing happened. pointerup now
   restores isPanning when exactly one pointer remains and re-anchors lastPan
   to that pointer, so the first move shifts by exactly its own delta.

2. [СР] The same applied after multitouch: three fingers down, two released,
   dx = 0.

3. [СР] zoomBy() with a non-finite factor poisoned the viewport permanently.
   Pinch computes factor = dist / lastPinchDist, which is Infinity when
   lastPinchDist is 0, and Math.max/min propagate NaN, so zoom/x/y became NaN
   and stayed NaN — the field stopped rendering until the app was restarted.
   Non-finite factors and anchor coordinates are now rejected, and a
   non-positive current zoom falls back to 1 rather than dividing by zero.

4. [Н] A third finger did not reset lastPinchDist, so a 3-finger gesture ran
   the 2-finger pinch branch with an arbitrary second point taken from Map
   insertion order — the zoom jumped when fingers were reordered.

Adds tests/renderer.gestures.test.mjs — 16 assertions driving the real
renderer with PointerEvent-shaped events at dpr = 2. Verified non-vacuous:
against the pre-fix source the suite fails 6 of 16.

Worth recording honestly: three earlier versions of the pinch assertion
failed against CORRECT code. The zoom anchor is the midpoint of the two
fingers (standard map behaviour), so (a) the midpoint is not stationary,
(b) the world point under the moving finger does shift — that finger is part
of the spread — and (c) the world point under the stationary finger shifts too
once the midpoint travels. The test now asserts the exact equivalence instead:
one pinch step == zoomBy(distance ratio, finger midpoint), plus a separate
check that a moving centre does drag the field.
