## 3. LLM Inference Optimization for AMD Hardware

### Key Findings

- **AMD 780M GPU** decision matrix established for local LLM inference:
  - **Ollama + ROCm:** Best all-around for AMD iGPU (qwen3, deepseek-coder, nemotron3.5 verified)
  - **llama.cpp + Vulkan:** Excellent for CPU/AMD/Apple Silicon with MTP speculative decoding
